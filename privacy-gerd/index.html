Privacy Policy

Reflux Relief · Effective November 18, 2026

The Short Version

Reflux Relief does not collect, store, share, or sell any of your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

Information You Enter

When you use Reflux Relief, you may record:

  • Symptom entries, including severity, symptoms, and possible triggers
  • Foods and meals you ate
  • Notes and the date and time of each entry

This information is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and we have no way to see it.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No name, email address, or account details. The app has no sign-up or login.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or Health app data
  • No usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No cookies or tracking across other apps and websites

The food database and tips are built into the app, so searching for foods works offline and does not send your searches anywhere.

Third Parties

Reflux Relief does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and does not share data with anyone.

Apple may provide app developers with aggregated, anonymous statistics (like download counts and crash reports) when you have opted in to share them in your device settings. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your device's backup may include Reflux Relief's data, just like other app data. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete any entry from the Log tab by swiping left on it. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children's Privacy

Reflux Relief does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data leaves your device, no personal information from children is collected.

Health Information

Reflux Relief is for general information and personal tracking only. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to your doctor about persistent symptoms.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If the app ever begins collecting data, we will update this policy before that change takes effect.
