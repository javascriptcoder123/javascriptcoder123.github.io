§1

Overview

Extractify Intel is a security checkup app for iPhone and iPad. It looks at your device's own settings and system state — things like your iOS version, passcode status, and system uptime — and turns them into a plain-English score and a list of things to fix.

There is no Extractify Intel server. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no analytics SDK bundled with the app. This policy exists because the App Store requires one, not because there's much to disclose.

§2

What Extractify Intel Looks At

To build your security score, Extractify Intel reads the following from your device, using standard iOS APIs available to any app:

  • Your installed iOS version, compared against a list built into the app
  • Whether a passcode and Face ID/Touch ID are enrolled (via Apple's LocalAuthentication framework — Extractify Intel never sees the passcode or biometric data itself, only a yes/no)
  • System uptime, to suggest a reboot when it's been a while
  • A small set of filesystem paths and loaded libraries, checked only for the presence of known jailbreak-tool artifacts
  • Answers you type or select yourself — such as whether Find My or Lockdown Mode is on, or how you'd describe recent device behavior — since iOS doesn't expose those settings to any app, including this one

None of this leaves your device. None of it is used to identify you.

§4

Where Your Answers Live

When you confirm something Extractify Intel can't check on its own — for example, tapping "Yes, it's enabled" for Find My — that answer is saved using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage, sandboxed to the Extractify Intel app on your device.

That storage is not synced to iCloud or any Extractify Intel-operated service, because no such service exists. It may be included in a standard encrypted device backup you make yourself (via iCloud Backup or a computer), the same as any other app's local data — that's an iOS platform behavior, not something Extractify Intel adds.

Deleting the app deletes these answers. There's no account to close and no server-side copy to request, because nothing ever leaves your device.
§5

Third Parties

Extractify Intel does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect data to share. It embeds no third-party SDKs — no analytics, no advertising, no cloud sync, no crash reporting. The app makes no network requests at all.

§6

Permissions

Extractify Intel's Face ID usage description exists so it can ask iOS whether biometric authentication is enrolled — it never actually prompts you for Face ID or Touch ID, and never receives biometric data. No other iOS permission (camera, contacts, location, network) is requested.

§7

Children's Privacy

Extractify Intel does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 — it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone at all, of any age, because it has no mechanism to collect or transmit it.

§8

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for instance, if a future version of Extractify Intel adds an optional feature that does involve network access — the "Effective" date at the top of this page will be updated, and material changes will be described in that version's App Store release notes before you update.
