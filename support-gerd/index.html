Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log symptoms?

Tap Log Symptoms on the Today screen, or the + button on the Log tab. Set the severity, tap any symptoms and possible triggers, add what you ate, and tap Save.

How do I edit or delete an entry?

On the Log tab, tap an entry to edit it. Swipe left on an entry to delete it.

How are foods rated?

Foods are rated Safe, Caution, or Trigger based on common dietary guidance for acid reflux and GERD. Everyone reacts differently, so use your symptom log to learn which foods affect you personally.

A food I searched for isn't listed.

Email us the food and we'll consider adding it in a future update. In the meantime, foods that are acidic, spicy, fatty, fried, minty, caffeinated, or carbonated are the most common triggers.

Where is my data stored?

Your symptom log is stored only on your device. Reflux Relief has no accounts and does not collect, share, or upload your data.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?

Yes. Because your data is stored only on your device, deleting the app also deletes your symptom log.

Medical Disclaimer

Reflux Relief is for general information and personal tracking only. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to your doctor about persistent symptoms.

Seek medical care right away for chest pain (especially with shortness of breath or pain in the arm or jaw), trouble swallowing, vomiting blood, or black stools. If you think you may be having a heart attack, call emergency services.
