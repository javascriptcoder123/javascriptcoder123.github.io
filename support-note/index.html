Frequently Asked

How do I get started with Note Essence?

Create an account at noteessence.com, verify your email, and your first notebook is ready to go. No setup required.

Are my notes backed up automatically?

Yes. Changes are saved continuously and synced across every device you're signed into.

Can I export my notes?

You can export individual notes or your entire library at any time from your account settings.

How do I reset my password?

Select "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen. A reset link will arrive in your inbox within a few minutes.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at admin@noteessence.com from your account address and we'll take care of it, exports included on request.

Something's broken — what should I include in my report?

Let us know what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help us fix it faster.
