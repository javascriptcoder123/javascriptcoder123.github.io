Privacy Policy

Companion Matched · Last updated August 10, 2026

This page explains what information Companion Matched collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. We wrote it to actually describe what the app does — not boilerplate.

Information we collect

Account & sign-in

Your email address and password (if you sign in with email), or the name and email Apple chooses to share when you use Sign in with Apple. Your password is handled entirely by our authentication provider (Supabase) — we never see or store it in plain text.

Profile information

What you add during setup and any time after: display name, username, birthdate, bio, profile photo, and the interests you select. Gender is optional.

Activity & content

Who you like or pass on, your matches, and the messages you send in chat. Messages are only ever visible to the two people in a match.

Device & notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device push token (via Apple Push Notification service) so we can notify you about new matches and messages.

What we don't collect

📍 No location data 📱 No phone number 🎯 No ad tracking or advertising IDs 🧩 No third-party analytics or ad SDKs

How we use it

  • Run the core features: showing you a Discover deck ranked by shared interests, creating matches, and powering chat.
  • Send you push notifications and account-related emails (confirming sign-up, password resets).
  • Keep the community safe — reviewing reports and enforcing blocks.
  • Maintain and improve the app (bug fixing, performance).

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

Who we share it with

WhoWhat for
SupabaseOur backend provider — hosts the database, authentication, file storage, and realtime messaging that run the app. They process data on our behalf and don't use it for their own purposes.
ApplePowers Sign in with Apple and delivers push notifications (APNs). Apple's handling of this data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.
Email delivery providerSends account emails (sign-up confirmation, password resets) on our behalf.

We don't share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party for marketing purposes.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at the address below — we'll remove your profile, matches, and messages.

Your choices

  • Edit or update your profile any time from the Profile tab.
  • Turn off push notifications in your device's Settings.
  • Block or report anyone directly from a chat — blocking removes them from your matches and Discover deck immediately.
  • Request a copy of your data or delete your account by contacting us.

Age requirement

Companion Matched is intended for users 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

Your data is protected by database-level Row Level Security — meaning access rules are enforced by the database itself, not just the app. Profile photos are stored so only you can write to your own folder, and chat messages are only readable by the two people in that match.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
